Falley is a physics game developed and published by Perf Games. This policy explains how the current offline game handles information, including optional motion controls.

Your game stays on your device. Falley has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. The game does not collect or send personal information to Perf Games.

1. Information stored on your device

Falley saves information in the app's private storage so it can remember your preferences and progress. This includes:

  • Language, motion controls and steering sensitivity settings.
  • Sound, vibration and blood effects preferences.
  • Selected stages, unlocked campaign progress, stars, earned appearance choices, arcade choices, scores, run counts and best times.
  • Daily Drop dates, attempts, results and completion streaks.

This information stays on your device. Perf Games cannot access it through the game.

2. Optional motion controls

When you enable motion steering, Falley uses available accelerometer and gyroscope readings to steer the character. These readings are processed temporarily in memory on your device. They are not saved as sensor history, uploaded or shared.

You can turn motion controls off in Settings and play using touch controls instead.

3. Permissions, sound and vibration

The current game requests no Android permissions, including no Internet permission. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location. Optional motion sensors do not require these permissions.

Sound effects are bundled with the game and played locally. Sound effects and vibration can be switched off in Settings. Falley does not record audio.

4. Collection, sharing and advertising

Perf Games does not collect, receive, sell or share personal information through this version of Falley. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, online accounts, online leaderboards, chat services or game servers.

If you choose Share Result after a Daily Drop, Falley creates a result image containing the course date, generator version, score, time, jump count, selected avatar and control mode. Android lets you choose an app to receive it. Nothing is shared automatically. The receiving app handles that image under its own policy.

Google Play and Android may separately process information when you install, update or review an app, under their own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Storage, security and deletion

Saved settings and records are held in Android's app-private storage. Falley disables Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup for this app. There is no Perf Games server copy of your game data.

Local records remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Falley. To delete them:

  1. Open your device's Settings → Apps → Falley.
  2. Choose Storage, then Clear storage or Clear data. The names may vary by device.

Uninstalling also removes the app's local records. Deletion resets your progress and settings and cannot be undone through Perf Games. There is no online account to delete.

A generated result image is kept temporarily in the app's private cache and replaced when you create another one. Android may clear this cache. Copies you choose to save or share in other apps must be deleted in those apps.

6. Visiting this policy website

This page has no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or embedded third-party content. The page itself does not set cookies or use browser storage.

Opening a website is separate from playing the offline game. This website is hosted on Vercel, which may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. See Vercel's Privacy Notice for its own practices.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current offline, ad-free version of Falley. If we introduce features that change how information is handled, such as ads or online services, we will update this policy before distributing a version with those changes. The date above identifies the latest update.
