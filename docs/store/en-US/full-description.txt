Guide a falling figure through elastic ropes, narrowing walls and incoming hazards. Time your jumps, steer through the gaps and make the landing.

PLAY WITH MOTION OR TOUCH
Tilt your phone to steer, or swipe left and right. Tap to jump. Choose Loose, Balanced or Tight motion response to find a comfortable feel. Touch play includes an air current that keeps you correcting your course.

LAND THE CAMPAIGN
Take on six unlocked stages across laser, spike and fire chapters. Bounce from ropes, build weight and drop toward a bouncy target. Later stages demand a precise landing.

BUILD YOUR SKIRMISH
Choose Chill, Standard or Brutal, then mix fireballs, top lasers, side lasers, diagonal lightning and buzzsaws. Read the warnings and survive for a better score and time.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
Sound effects, vibration, motion response and blood effects can be adjusted in Settings. Campaign and Arcade records stay on your device.
