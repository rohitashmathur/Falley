This beta is offline, ad-free and has no account or purchases. It includes stylized death and blood effects; Blood Effects can be switched off in Settings. Android 7.0 or newer is required. Motion uses available phone sensors, and touch controls also work.

Join using the Google account approved for this test:

Send feedback through [[FEEDBACK_CHANNEL]]. Device model, Android version and a short description are enough. A screen recording is optional; avoid including notifications or other people's information.
