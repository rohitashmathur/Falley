Falley is a physics game developed and published by Perf Games. Play works offline. This policy explains local data and Google Firebase reporting and gameplay input analysis in version 4.4.1.

Reporting starts on. Crash reports and gameplay analytics are enabled by default when no choice is saved. Analytics includes sampled motion and touch input during gameplay. Turn either off in Settings > Diagnostics & analytics. Saved opt-outs are preserved. You can play with both off.

1. Offline play and optional reporting

Falley 4.4.1 can be played without an Internet connection. Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, are on by default in debug and release builds when no reporting choice has been saved. A previously saved opt-out is kept. You can turn either service off separately in Settings > Diagnostics & analytics. Neither is required to play. With both options off when the app starts, Falley does not initialize Firebase. Falley has no accounts, ads or purchases.

2. Motion controls

Motion steering processes accelerometer and gyroscope readings on your device. While Gameplay analytics is on, active gameplay also samples the unfiltered X/Y/Z accelerometer and gyroscope values, at most twice per second for each sensor, plus device rotation and time since the run began. Gameplay touch paths include view-relative pixel coordinates, touch phases, screen dimensions and relative times; move samples are limited to ten per second, with press/release/cancel boundaries also recorded. Completed steering swipes include start/end coordinates and duration. Sensors are sampled for reporting only while motion play is active. Falley does not collect input from other apps, keyboard text or sensors while the game is paused or in the background.

3. Country and language

Falley may read a coarse country hint from your mobile network, SIM provider or device region to assist language selection. This is not precise location. That hint is temporary, is not saved, and is not included in Falley reporting events. English is selected by default; you choose a language on first launch and can change it in Settings. If you enable Analytics, Google may separately derive approximate geography from network information such as your IP address.

4. Data on your device

Falley saves preferences, reporting choices, selected stages, campaign unlocks, stars, avatar and rope choices, scores, run counts, best times, Daily Drop results and streaks in app-private storage. Music, sound effects and vibration are produced locally. Your full save file is not uploaded. If you enable reporting, the limited diagnostic or gameplay information described below can be sent to Google for Perf Games.

5. Optional crash reports

When enabled, Firebase Crashlytics sends crash stack traces and related diagnostics, app version, device and operating-system information, installation identifiers, and limited game state such as mode, stage, wave, avatar, controls and outcome. Perf Games uses these reports to find and fix faults. When Analytics is also enabled, event breadcrumbs, including sampled gameplay inputs, may accompany crash reports. We do not assign your name, email address or an account ID to reports. Changes to crash reporting fully apply on the next app launch. Enabling it can upload crash information stored locally while Firebase was active earlier, including when only Analytics was enabled. Reports already queued or in transit may still finish sending after you turn it off. Turning it off does not delete reports already received.

6. Optional gameplay analytics

When Gameplay analytics is on, Falley records run starts and ends, mode, stage, wave, difficulty, avatar, control mode, outcome, jump counts, duration ranges and hazard category. Individual jump attempts include acceptance, relative time and avatar position. It also records completed swipes, sampled raw motion and touch paths, menu screen transitions, language changes, newly unlocked avatars, rope landings/cuts and rope type, and Share Result button taps. A share tap does not prove that an image was sent. A random identifier groups events within one run. The SDK can also collect automatic app/session events, app-install/source attribution, timestamps, app version, device/OS details, language, an app-instance identifier and approximate geography. Perf Games uses this information to study controls, difficulty and navigation. Falley disables advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization. Names, emails, the full save file and Daily date/seed are not included in custom analytics. Analytics can be turned off independently of crash reports.

7. Your choices

Both reporting options start on when no choice is saved. Change them separately in Settings > Diagnostics & analytics; saved opt-outs are preserved across updates. Analytics changes apply immediately: turning it off discards pending in-memory input batches and asks the SDK to reset local Analytics data. Crash-report changes fully apply on the next app launch. Input is grouped into at most ten samples per event; up to 192 events plus partial sample groups wait in memory. A background worker hands batches to Firebase about every 15 seconds, at run end and when the app pauses. Firebase controls offline storage, retries and actual upload timing. No sensors are recorded in the background. Pending memory-only data can be lost if the process is killed; oldest queued events are dropped when the buffer fills. Already handed-off or in-flight data may still arrive, and turning reporting off does not remove information already received by Google. Gameplay remains available with both options off.

8. Deleting your data

Clear Falley app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to erase local preferences and progress. This cannot be undone; Android app backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Turning reporting off or clearing local storage does not erase data already received by Google. Analytics retention follows the Google Analytics property settings and applicable deletion procedures. Google currently documents Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers as retained for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Contact us for a privacy request; without an account or identifying details, we may be unable to link a request to a particular pseudonymous report.

9. Google services and network access

Optional reporting uses Google Firebase and Google Analytics. Google processes information under the applicable Firebase/Analytics terms and privacy information; processing may take place outside your country. Perf Games can view the resulting reporting dashboards. Falley uses Internet, network-state, background wake and Play install-referrer access permissions for these services. It does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access. Music and effects are bundled locally and Falley does not record audio. Read Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase details at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/ .

10. Sharing a result

Daily Drop records, stars, streaks and appearance choices are saved on your device. If you choose Share Result, Falley creates an image with the course date, generator version, score, time, jumps, avatar and control mode. Android lets you choose where to share it. No result image is shared automatically. The receiving app handles the image under its own policy. A temporary copy remains in app-private cache; copies saved or shared elsewhere must be deleted there. If Gameplay analytics is on, tapping Share Result is recorded as a usage event, without the image, recipient or destination app.

11. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your inquiry or privacy request. Gmail, provided by Google, handles correspondence under Google's Privacy Policy. We keep messages only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, unless law requires longer. You can request deletion by email. We do not sell messages or use them for advertising. Contacting us does not automatically send game records or sensor readings. Do not include sensitive information.

The policy page has no analytics scripts, forms, advertising or external assets. Vercel hosts it and may process technical request information such as an IP address to deliver and protect the page. This website visit is separate from your in-game reporting choices.
