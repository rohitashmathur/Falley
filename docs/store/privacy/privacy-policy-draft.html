Falley Privacy Policy

Effective date: [[POLICY_EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

Falley is an Android game branded Perf Games and published by [[LEGAL_PUBLISHER_NAME]]. This policy describes the offline, ad-free beta, version 3.2.0, package com.perfgames.falley.

What the game handles

Falley does not require an account. The game does not transmit personal information, usage analytics or gameplay data to Perf Games or third parties. This beta includes no advertising or analytics SDK and requests no Internet permission.

The game stores settings and gameplay records on your device, including selected stages and Arcade hazards, motion response, sound, vibration and blood preferences, scores, survival times and campaign finishes. These records are used to remember your choices and show your progress.

Motion controls

If motion steering is enabled and the sensors are available, accelerometer and gyroscope readings are processed in memory on your device to steer the character. Falley does not record or transmit sensor history. You can switch motion steering off and use touch controls.

Sound and vibration

Sound effects are bundled with the game and played locally. Sound and vibration can be switched off in Settings. No microphone recording is performed.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local settings and records remain in the app's storage until you clear that storage or uninstall Falley. Use Android Settings, Apps, Falley, Storage, Clear storage; names may differ by device. Uninstalling also removes these local records. Android app backup is disabled for this build. Perf Games has no server copy of these game records to restore or delete.

Sharing and online services

The game itself does not sell or share your personal information and has no online leaderboard, chat, backend or in-app data-upload feature. Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own policies when you install, update or review apps.

When you contact us separately

If you choose to contact the publisher outside the game, the publisher receives the information you provide, such as your email address, message and optional device details, to respond to your inquiry. Do not send sensitive personal information.
