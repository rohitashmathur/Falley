## Remaining app and Console work

1. Enter the production policy URL and monitored support email above in the applicable Play Console fields. No Console settings have been changed by recording these values here.
2. Use the new **3.3.1/code 11** local release described in [privacy update notes](../../PRIVACY_UPDATE_3_3_1.md). It includes the real contact, public policy URL and browser/email actions in Settings > Privacy Policy. Older 3.3.0/code 10 binaries still have the no-contact text and are superseded for this purpose; website publication alone does not update installed apps.
3. Confirm the final artifact remains offline, with no ads, analytics, accounts or data-upload feature, and matches the sensor, local-storage and deletion statements. Keep audience, content rating, Data safety and real-phone acceptance as separate owner/release tasks.
4. Monitor the public mailbox and follow the published support-message handling commitments. Keep app/publisher branding consistent with the real verified Console identity; the site does not establish legal account verification.
5. Keep the URL accessible without a password, login or country restriction and update the policy before distributing any build whose data practices change.

Google requires a policy link in Console and policy text or a link in the app, including apps that do not access personal/sensitive data. It must identify the app/publisher, explain handling/retention/deletion, offer a contact mechanism, and use a public non-geofenced non-editable-by-viewers URL rather than a PDF. [Play User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en)

This beta has no AdMob or UMP. If ads, analytics, accounts or online feedback are added later, re-audit and revise the policy and declarations **before** distributing that build. Consent text cannot simply be copied from this offline policy.
