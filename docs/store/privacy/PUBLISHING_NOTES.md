# Privacy policy publication: not complete

`privacy-policy-draft.html` is an editable local draft for the **offline, ad-free 3.2.0 beta**. It is not a public policy and must not be entered as a local path in Console. Every `[[...]]` token is an unresolved owner input.

Before publishing:

1. Confirm the actual legal publisher behind Perf Games and supply a reachable, monitored privacy/support email. Use that identity consistently with Console. The owner has explicitly said contact/site are not ready.
2. Decide how voluntarily submitted support messages will be retained and handled. Complete that section rather than claiming a retention period no one has adopted.
3. Check the final AAB remains offline, has no analytics/ads/accounts/upload feature, and matches the policy's local sensor, storage and deletion statements.
4. Replace all placeholders, remove the draft warning and draft labels in the title/footer, and review the finished page. Host it at a stable public HTTPS URL accessible worldwide. An owned static hosting service can work; a custom domain is not mandatory. No hosting has been configured or published here.
5. Enter the real policy URL in Play Console. The shipped Settings > Privacy Policy text should agree with it and include a real contact mechanism once available; the current no-contact offline screen is still a policy-completeness gap for submission.

Google requires a policy link in Console and policy text or a link in the app, including apps that do not access personal/sensitive data. It must identify the app/publisher, explain handling/retention/deletion, offer a contact mechanism, and use a public non-geofenced non-editable-by-viewers URL rather than a PDF. [Play User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en)

This beta has no AdMob or UMP. If ads, analytics, accounts or online feedback are added later, re-audit and revise the policy and declarations **before** distributing that build. Consent text cannot simply be copied from this offline policy.
