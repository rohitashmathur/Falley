# Falley: Google Play and advertising readiness

Reviewed 15 October 2026 for a worldwide Google Play release. Repository: `rohitashmathur/Falley`; application ID: `com.perfgames.falley`. This report distinguishes source observations, researched requirements and product recommendations. No Play Console or AdMob account was accessed, no advertising SDK was added, and no store submission was made.

**Decision: continue local playtesting, then prepare a Play internal-test release. Not ready for an advertising-supported production launch.** The source already targets API 36. The principal gaps are a validated release-signed AAB, real-device acceptance testing, privacy/store declarations, and an implemented and tested advertising/consent flow. The 3.1.0/code 6 audio and buzzsaw changes passed independent source review; their exact build/test result is recorded in `BUILD_STATUS.md`.

## Verified publishing requirements

1. **Target API:** Google now requires new mobile apps and updates to target Android 16/API 36 or higher, effective 31 August 2026. Falley's manifest and Gradle configuration meet that target. Recheck the actual release artifact and policy at submission; this report does not predict later mandates. [Google Play target API policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878?hl=en)

2. **Bundle and signing:** new Play apps publish using an Android App Bundle. Configure Play App Signing and sign uploads with a secured upload key. The debug key is unsuitable for production. Preserve keys/passwords outside Git and keep AAB/APK files local under the repository publication policy. [Android App Bundles](https://developer.android.com/guide/app-bundle), [app signing](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing)

3. **Account and verification:** confirm the correct personal/organization account type, legal identity/contact information, required device verification and package registration status in the owner's Console. New account registration has a US$25 one-time fee. Account type, creation date, production access, payments setup and verification status are unknown here. [Play Console setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435?hl=en), [developer identity verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920?hl=en), [Android developer verification](https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16561738?hl=en)

4. **Conditional closed test:** personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days before applying for production access. This is not a universal prerequisite for every account type. Completing the time/count threshold leads to an application, not automatic production approval; retain actual feedback and fixes. Internal testing is a useful first step. [Testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en)

5. **16 KB compatibility:** Java/Kotlin-only applications, including all libraries, already support 16 KB page-size devices, although Android recommends testing. Falley currently contains no native libraries. Re-audit the final bundle after adding an advertising SDK or mediation adapter; do not assume the current source result covers new dependencies. [Android page-size guidance](https://developer.android.com/guide/practices/page-sizes)

## Violence, audience and monetization

**Researched facts:** Play generally allows fictional cartoon violence in games but prohibits gratuitous violence and graphic depictions/descriptions of realistic violence. Passing automated tests does not settle the judgment about the final audiovisual presentation. [Play violence policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18258653?hl=en)

IARC authorities assign regional ratings from accurate questionnaire answers; ratings may differ between territories and can affect availability. Declare all accessible blood, impalement, burning, bone and saw effects and the new sounds. Choosing an intended audience is distinct from receiving a content rating. Including children in the target audience triggers Families obligations; ads and offers must also match the assigned rating. Do not promise a particular age rating or worldwide acceptance before the questionnaire and review. [Content ratings and target audience](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655?hl=en)

**Recommendation:** position this presentation for a truthful older audience, with no child-directed marketing. If retaining the graphic deaths, assess an adult-focused launch rather than labelling the game as suitable for everyone. Keep deaths brief and visibly fictional. Review spike-pinning and saw reactions especially for prolonged suffering. Any decision to soften effects should be a product decision, not a way to give incomplete declarations.

**Important AdMob distinction:** ordinary violent gameplay is not automatically subject to the shocking-content advertising restriction. Google's gameplay-imagery exception singles out torture, sexual violence, violence against minors or prominent real people, and violence based on protected/marginalized characteristics. Restricted inventory can have fewer eligible advertisers or no ads. Falley's figure has no age established in the source; an adult character design and brief accidental deaths would be a better fit for this exception than child characters or realistic suffering. No approval or revenue is guaranteed. [Google Publisher Restrictions](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10437795?hl=en)

Store metadata must be appropriate for a general audience; graphic violence prominently shown in icons, promotional images or videos is prohibited. Use genuine gameplay showing ropes, dodging and targets without blood/impalement closeups. The store listing should still accurately describe the action and assigned rating. [Play metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en)

## Privacy: current offline build versus future ads

**Current source assessment:** no data is transmitted off device by the inspected implementation. Local sensor processing and local records do not count as collection for the Play Data safety form. Subject to checking the final artifact, a no-collection/no-sharing declaration is consistent with this offline build. Apps exclusively on internal testing are exempt from the Data safety form; closed/open/production distribution requires completion even when no user data is collected. [Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)

All apps still need a privacy policy in Play Console and policy text or a link within the app. It must identify the app/publisher, explain handling/retention/deletion and provide a contact mechanism. The URL must be public, accessible worldwide, non-editable by viewers and not a PDF. Current deletion is clear app data/uninstall; there is no account whose deletion must be implemented. [Play User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en)

**After ads:** do not carry the offline declaration forward unchanged. Google Mobile Ads documentation lists automatic handling of IP addresses, interaction/diagnostic data and device/account identifiers. Map the chosen SDK/version, settings and any mediation partners to actual data types, purposes, collection and sharing. Update the policy and declarations before distributing that build; non-personalized ads are not synonymous with zero data processing. [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

For EEA/UK/Switzerland personalized advertising, Google requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. Google's UMP is a suitable implementation route. Create the messages in AdMob, refresh consent information each launch, show required forms, check `canRequestAds()` before loading ads, and expose privacy choices whenever required. Test accept, refuse, reopen/withdraw, expired consent, offline/error and reinstall cases. Do not reduce compliance to checking a country once. [CMP requirements](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/13554020?hl=en), [UMP integration](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy)

Configure applicable US state privacy choices and the UMP/GPP or restricted-data-processing path. A European consent form alone is insufficient for a worldwide advertising plan. Determine the applicable jurisdictional obligations for the publisher and intended audience before launch. [Google's US states guidance](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/us-states)

## Recommended first advertising design

**Recommendation, not an implemented feature:** begin with one interstitial placement at an established run-end transition, after the result is understood and before returning to the menu, with a conservative time/run cap. Keep the first sessions ad-free. Falley runs can be short, so an ad after every death would overwhelm play. Do not interrupt the fall, hazard warnings, jump/swipe input, splash or the start action. Avoid a banner near the full-width jump control.

Google recommends interstitials at natural pauses, and Play prohibits unexpected full-screen interruptions at gameplay/content beginnings. Pause simulation, unregister/recenter motion input and stop sound during full-screen ads; return to a paused/result state after dismissal or failure. An unavailable ad must never block restarting or playing offline. [Interstitial guidance](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/interstitial), [Play ads policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9857753?hl=en)

Rewarded ads can be added later only when a meaningful reward is defined. A clear opt-in could offer one unranked practice continuation per run; separate assisted records if chosen. This is a design proposal, not existing progression. Clearly state the reward before the ad, honor completion once, and keep normal play available when declined. [Rewarded-ad rules](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7313578?hl=en)

## Ordered publication checklist

1. **Finish the 3.1.0 acceptance pass:** verify audio for fire, lasers, lightning and saws; warning-safe/active saw collisions; configuration persistence; pause/background/restart cleanup; sound-off behavior and mixed-hazard fairness. Record the exact APK checksum and results in `BUILD_STATUS.md`. Add phone sessions on low/mid/high-end devices and at least one device without a gyro, with both motion and touch.
2. **Create the release toolchain:** add a pinned Gradle wrapper, resolve/test dependencies, create a non-debug `bundleRelease` path, secure the upload key, validate the AAB and install its generated APKs. Confirm API 36, package ID, a fresh version code, non-debuggable manifest, absence of instrumentation/test resources and no private files. Store binaries and keys locally.
3. **Prepare publisher identity and support:** confirm Play/AdMob account ownership, verification and payment/tax requirements in those accounts; choose support email and public developer website. Host the privacy policy and later `app-ads.txt` on that site. No account-specific status was verified in this review.
4. **Implement the ads/consent release:** choose a currently supported Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP, add AdMob app/ad-unit IDs and required merged-manifest entries, enforce consent/privacy decisions, handle failures, and protect gameplay lifecycle/audio. Review every added SDK/adapter and its disclosures. Use demo ad units or registered test devices throughout development; do not click live ads while testing. [SDK setup](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/quick-start), [test ads](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/test-ads)
5. **Complete AdMob setup:** create/link the app to its supported-store listing when available, publish the supplied authorized-seller line at the developer website root `app-ads.txt`, request verification and obtain app-readiness approval. New apps require this verification before fully serving ads; successful integration alone does not enable full serving. [App setup](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9989980?hl=en), [app-ads.txt setup](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9363762?hl=en), [verification and readiness](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/14538460?hl=en)
6. **Complete Play app content:** privacy URL, accurate Data safety, Contains ads = Yes for the advertising build (No for the current build), advertising-ID declaration as applicable to the merged manifest/SDK, truthful target audience, content rating and app-access instructions. The current game needs no reviewer login. Check account/territory-specific Console tasks rather than marking unknown items complete. [Policy declarations](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/app-store-review/policy-declarations)
7. **Prepare the store listing:** app name, concise descriptions, support details, clean icon export, 1024x500 feature graphic and representative phone screenshots. Use non-gory frames and accurate motion-control wording; optional sensors must remain optional. Meet the current asset requirements shown by Console. [Store preview requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en)
8. **Run internal then closed testing:** install through Play, verify clean install/update from a prior Play build, inspect pre-launch crashes/ANRs/accessibility, exercise consent regions and no-fill/no-network scenarios, and collect feedback on motion, difficulty and ad frequency. Complete the 12-testers/14-days route if that account requires it. Debug-key installs may need uninstalling to replace them with the differently signed Play version; preserve feedback before clearing local data.
9. **Production gate:** all release blockers closed, rating/declarations match the shipped build, ads/consent and account/app approvals confirmed, real-phone acceptance documented, and ownership of code/name/art/audio established. Submit the reviewed release when authorized, select permitted territories, and expand deliberately while monitoring Android vitals, reviews and AdMob Policy Center. Do not promise that “worldwide” guarantees every territory permits this rating/content or provides advertising demand.

Keep APK/AAB files, signing material and account credentials out of GitHub. AdMob app/ad-unit IDs are public identifiers rather than account credentials; use test units in development. This report does not perform advertising-account changes or store publication.
