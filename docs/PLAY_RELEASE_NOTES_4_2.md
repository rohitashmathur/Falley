## English (en-US)

- Choose English, Hindi or Bahasa Indonesia on first launch.

- New avatar jump animations and jump previews.
- Earn Spring and Static ropes with unique campaign effects.
- Original retro music with a separate on/off switch.
- Cleaner Collection, faster retries and optimized app size.
